01 / THE PROBLEM

The Multi-Platform Statement Chaos

Modern traders don't trade on one platform. You may use live broker accounts, prop firm accounts, futures platforms, and payout rails like Rise, Deel, Wise, Payoneer, or crypto exchanges, leaving you and your accountant with mismatched CSVs, receipts, and transaction fees.

RECONCILIATION CHALLENGE THE MESSY MANUAL WAY THE TRADE LEDGER WAY
Prop Firm Payouts Handing your CPA 15 separate Deel/Rise invoices with missing original account references. Automated payout matching linking prop firm IDs, original USD payouts, and local currency bank deposits.
Forex & Futures Brokers Combining broker CSV exports and futures contract notes in custom Excel sheets. Editable normalization layer extracting tickets, commission, swap, and net closed P&L from accepted source files.
Exchange Fees & Spreads Manually separating gross payouts, fees, rollover, and swap lines across several sources. Fee and swap lines are organized into reference categories for accountant review.
Accountant Handoff CPAs charging $500-$2,000 extra just to sort through raw screenshots and unstructured PDFs. One-click export of a standardized 4-tab Excel reconciliation pack for accountant review or self-filing.
